Security and privacy are often treated as separate concerns — one for sysadmins, one for activists. But they're two sides of the same coin.

## The Connection

When you secure your devices, you're also limiting who can observe you. When you encrypt your communications, you're ensuring only the intended recipient can read them. Every security measure is, in some form, a privacy measure.

## Practical Takeaways

- **Patch your systems.** Unpatched vulnerabilities are the most common attack vector. Keep your OS and software updated.
- **Use strong, unique passwords.** A password manager makes this easy — check the guides section for a walkthrough.
- **Enable two-factor authentication.** Even if your password is compromised, 2FA adds a critical second barrier.
- **Encrypt your storage.** Full-disk encryption means that if your device is stolen, your data stays yours.

Security isn't about paranoia. It's about taking reasonable steps to ensure that your digital life remains yours.
