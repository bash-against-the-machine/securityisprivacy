Reusing passwords across sites is one of the most common and dangerous security mistakes. When one site is breached, all your accounts with the same password become vulnerable. A password manager solves this.

## Step 1: Choose a Password Manager

Good options include:

- **Bitwarden** — open-source, free tier is excellent, cross-platform
- **1Password** — polished UX, paid, great for teams
- **KeePassXC** — fully offline, open-source, stores your vault locally

For most people, **Bitwarden** is the best starting point: it's free, open-source, and syncs across devices.

## Step 2: Create Your Master Password

Your master password is the one password you must remember. Make it:

- At least 16 characters
- A passphrase works well: `correct-horse-battery-staple`
- Never used anywhere else

## Step 3: Install the Browser Extension

Bitwarden has extensions for Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Once installed, it will auto-fill credentials on sites you've saved.

## Step 4: Migrate Your Existing Passwords

Start by saving passwords as you log in to sites normally. Over time, replace any reused or weak passwords with randomly generated ones (let the manager generate them — use at least 20 characters).

## Step 5: Enable Two-Factor Authentication on the Vault

Protect the manager itself with 2FA. Use an authenticator app (not SMS) for the strongest protection.

---

Once set up, you'll never need to remember a password again — except the one master password.
