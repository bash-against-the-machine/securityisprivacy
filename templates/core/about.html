About

Who I Am

Welcome to SecurityisPrivacy — a resource dedicated to cybersecurity education, practical how-to guides, and staying informed about the evolving threat landscape.

I believe that security knowledge shouldn't be locked behind paywalls or jargon. Whether you're hardening your own systems, learning to think like an attacker, or just trying to protect your digital privacy — this site is for you.

What You'll Find Here

  • ▸ How-To Guides — practical, step-by-step walkthroughs on security topics
  • ▸ Blog — opinions, deep dives, and analysis
  • ▸ News — curated security news and vulnerability updates

Philosophy

Privacy is not a luxury — it's a right. And security is how you exercise it. The tools, knowledge, and awareness shared here are meant to empower individuals and organizations to take control of their own digital safety.

"Security is not a product, but a process."

Bruce Schneier
